We were all really excited to host Scott Rodlin and Becca Cooper of Paragon Jousting for a wonderfully educational presentation this morning.  Scott spoke at length about the life and goals of a knight, all while suiting up for "battle" and answering the slew of questions that came his way.  It was a lot of fun and very memorable.
