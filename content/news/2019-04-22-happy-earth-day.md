“The more clearly we can focus our attention on the wonders and realities of the universe about us, the less taste we shall have for destruction.”   ― Rachel Carson

  

We celebrated Earth Day with our annual lake shore clean up.  Things looked pretty clean, but we still wound up with one bag of recyclables and two bags of trash.  The weather was gorgeous and we finished our morning by mulching the playground.  Happy Earth Day!
