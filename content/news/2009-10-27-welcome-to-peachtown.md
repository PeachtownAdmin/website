Welcome to the new website of Peachtown Elementary school!  
  
This site is a work in progress, but in the coming days and weeks you can expect to find information about our educational philosophy and program, our teachers, and our students.  
  
We also plan to have a running blog, which will chronicle our year and help you get to know us better.  
  
On this site you can find forms to apply for admission and scholarship, as well as information on financing a Peachtown education.  
  
We hope you enjoy getting to know us - and we look forward to getting to know you. Please don't hesitate to contact us with your questions or concerns.
