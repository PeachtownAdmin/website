Every year, Peachtown students celebrate Earth Day by clearing the Wells College lake shore of trash and other debris. This year their efforts were part of the *Embrace the Lake* *Campaign* sponsored by the Cayuga Lake Watershed Network and the Center for Environmental Sustainability. This project hopes to organize clean-ups of all the major creeks that empty into Cayuga Lake.
