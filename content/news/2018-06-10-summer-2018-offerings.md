**Peachtown Elementary School Summer Offerings**

*All camps are full day and run from 9AM – 4PM. Early drop off and late pick up will be made available as needed for a small additional fee. Cost per week is $175 and there is a minimum of 5 children needed to run each week.*  
\>>>      [REGISTRATION FORM](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Nwn5hDuODtpC8jdj7mB5jBW4PbeKcrC-/view?usp=sharing)     <<<

  

**June 18-22**

**Main Lesson Enrichment Week, Grades PreK through 8**

A collection of daily trips to explore topics introduced throughout the school year.

  

**June 25-29**

**Café Fun, Grades PreK through 3**

Math and writing will be incorporated into this fun exploration of food, both real and pretend.

  

**July 30-August 3**

**Fun with Comic Books and Graphic Novels, Grades PreK through 3**

Children will read, write, and draw as they learn to create their own graphic novels or comic books.

  

**August 13-17**

**LEGO Robotics, Grades 4-8**

Older children will learn to build, code, and program using LEGO robotics kits. The week will finish with a set of challenges for the robots to accomplish.
