We are a little sad to announce that Kathy Button will be retiring at the end of this school year. She has worked tirelessly for 11 years in the primary classroom, and she will be sorely missed by her students and her colleagues. Fortunately, we were able to find someone that we think will fill Kathy's shoes beautifully. Alyssa Binns Gunderson will join our staff in the fall. She brings a wonderful variety of teaching, administrative and community action experience to the classroom. She is a wonderful scholar and communicator with a lovely style of teaching. We are excited to begin our planning this summer!
