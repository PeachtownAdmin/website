THROUGHOUT THE MONTH OF NOVEMBER, STUDENTS AT PEACHTOWN HAVE BEEN LEARNING ABOUT ANCIENT MESOPOTAMIA. AS PART OF THAT STUDY THE PRIMARY CLASSROOM STUDENTS SEARCHED THROUGH SHOE-BOX MIDDENS, CAREFULLY SORTING AND ANALYZING THEIR FINDINGS. UPPER CLASSROOM STUDENTS RECONSTRUCTED FOUND SHARDS BACK INTO THEIR ORIGINAL FORMS.
