The Peachtown community is saddened by the death of Charlie Snyder. Charlie served Peachtown well as a Board Member, volunteer and longstanding contributor. His steady guidance helped move the school from a nascent idealistic organization into the stable effective school it is today. He took part in activities as diverse as walking a piano for the school up Route 90, to cutting out walls for expanded classrooms, to acting as a thoughtful advisor on many board committees. He will be sorely missed as a friend, colleague and role model. He was a "peach" of a man!
