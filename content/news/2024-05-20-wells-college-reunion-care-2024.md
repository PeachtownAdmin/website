[Please use this link](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xIrIlzpHPimVawpP1mdHunEXdQ9NiWSgHhGSUc_jS7I/edit?usp=sharing) to register your child(ren) for care on Friday, May 31 and/or Saturday, June 1.  Payment, which is accepted in cash, check to Peachtown Elementary, or [PayPal](http://www.peachtownschool.com/p/payments-gifts.html) will be accepted as a donation to Peachtown Elementary School.  Funds will help support our plan to work with the College and the Attorney General's office to stay in our current location between Glen Park and the Medical Center on Dean's Road.  

Peachtown was founded by Wells Alum, Barb Post '78 and continues to be staffed by several alumnae spanning several decades.  Over the past 32 years we have treasured our relationship with the Wells community.  We hope that we have given as much as we have received.

To have and to share.  Always.
