We had such a great time bringing our Renaissance unit to life!  Everyone pitched in to help prepare the building and to test crafts and materials before the big day.  Our musicians all made beautiful music, and the community really came out in support of our little school.  Thank you to everyone who helped to make our event a success!  We'd like to give a special thanks to the Aurora Preschool, the Hazard Library, Jane Morgan's Little House, Bill Hitchcock, Lili Mac Cormick, and the Aurora Shoe Co. for sharing their time, materials, and talents with us.
