**

**The 4 A’s**

**Autism, Allergies, Asthma, ADHD: An Integrative Approach to Your Child’s Diagnosis and Treatment**

Peachtown Elementary School in Aurora will sponsor a presentation and discussion at the Auburn Public Theater at 6:00 PM on June 23. The free program focuses on an improved understanding of the diagnoses and possible treatments of autism, allergies, asthma and ADHD.

**

Dr. Marne O’Shea, an Ithaca area Family Practice M.D., and Stacey Weeks-Purdy, an Integrative Nutrition Counselor certified by the AADP, will discuss the diagnoses of these disorders and how treatment plans can be improved with an approach that integrates changes in diet and nutrition. Ideas for shopping and preparing foods are provided along with samples to taste!

The program is made possible by a grant from the Cayuga Community Fund.
