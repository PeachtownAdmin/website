It's the start of another great year at Peachtown!  We're happy to welcome Lower Classroom Head Teacher, Michele Lawson to our staff this year.  She holds degrees in Psychology and Education and has a lot of experience working in both traditional and Montessori settings.  Michele has enjoyed getting to know our youngest students and is doing a great job transitioning to our progressive multi-age classroom model.  Welcome, Michele!
