Our recent outing to the Museum of the Earth was a wonderful experience.  The younger children had a full tour through all the eras of geologic history (given by Peachtown alum, Johanna Batman), explored all the hands-on exhibits and enjoyed the “Do Dinosaurs Poop?” exhibit.  The older students added to their experience as they explored the small, but eclectic art exhibit with Marilyn, had a dinosaur cartooning class with paleo-artist Richard Kissel, and a presentation on the different aspects of anthropology and an overview of the evolution of hominids.
