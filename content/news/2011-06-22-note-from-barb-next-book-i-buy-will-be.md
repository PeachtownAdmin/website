Note from Barb:

  

The next book I buy will be *The Blessings of a B-Minus: Using Jewish Teachings to Raise Resilient Teenagers,* by Wendy Mogel. Based on the review in the New York Times and a quick clip I saw on television, Mogel's approach to raising adolescents sounds very wise. She even includes one of my favorite adages as a handy rule for parenting: put the oxygen mask on yourself before you put it on the child.

  

Check it out. [http://parenting.blogs.nytimes.com/2010/10/12/the-blessings-of-a-b-minus/#more-15889](http://parenting.blogs.nytimes.com/2010/10/12/the-blessings-of-a-b-minus/#more-15889)
