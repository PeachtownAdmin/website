### Small
With an enrollment of 25 students and a student teacher ratio of 6:1, Peachtown offers a highly individualized educational program.  Based in a former home, our campus is warm and inviting.  Our students and teachers form a tight-knit and supportive community that, combined with a multi-age approach, encourages experiential learning, mutual respect, and a sense of accountability. 

  

### Intelligent
Our curriculum is carefully crafted to be holistic and multidimensional with a focus on developmentally appropriate activities.  We adhere to a no-testing policy and firmly believe that true learning is demonstrated in a multitude of ways.  We are pleased to offer [French and Spanish](/world-languages/) courses in addition to [Art](/fine-arts/) and [Music](/music/) instruction.

  

### Connected

Located on the [Wells College](http://wells.edu/) campus, in the quaint [village of Aurora](http://auroranewyork.us/), we have access to many wonderful opportunities that belie our rural setting.  Primary students make weekly trips to the [public library](http://www.aurorafreelibrary.org/), and when Wells was open, enjoyed regular swim and gym classes in the Wells College athletic center.  We are hopeful that the next owners of the Wells campus will work with us to offer the same opportunities in the future.  

  

### Affordable

We believe that a great education should be affordable and accessible.  Our [tuition](/admissions/#tuition-and-scholarships) remains affordable at  $7,500 per academic year and our students come from a wide range of socio-economic backgrounds, with up to 50% of families receiving need-based [scholarships](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PzkKMUetQVJeWNW0rItj57oqXe1jYWbc6yIRgwXjVKM/edit?usp=sharing).  We are pleased to offer various interest-free payment plans for our families and have free busing for students who qualify based on home address.  We have a four day school week, with classes in session from Monday through Thursday.  After school care is also provided for a nominal fee.

## Tuition and scholarships

With an annual tuition of $7,500,  Peachtown is committed to offering private education at an affordable price.  We are pleased to provide need-based scholarships for families who qualify and a 15% discount for siblings who attend concurrently.  You can read more about our tuition and the [financing of a Peachtown education here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11i0FsGu9_zEwfzV26MefTrx7l1KzDEKSvD3pdv3RuPw/edit?usp=sharing).
