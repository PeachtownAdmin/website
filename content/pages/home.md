Peachtown Elementary School is an independent non-sectarian day school for
grades Pre-K through 8, located in Aurora, New York. With a small enrollment
and a low student teacher ratio, Peachtown offers a highly individualized
program built on multi-age classrooms, hands-on learning, world languages,
and the creative and performing arts.

Peachtown was founded in 1990.
