At Peachtown, physical education emphasizes learning to integrate fitness into everyday life. During PE, students learn cooperative play and team building while developing knowledge of exercise for overall health.  Students of all ages have a full hour for lunch and play in addition to weekly PE courses.  Students enjoy the outdoors in all but the most inclement weather. Lower Level students walk the mile to and from the public library each week. Winter months find students sledding and building snow structures, and warm days entice teachers and students to benefit from our large outdoor "gym". The multi-age nature of the classes fosters team spirit, cooperation and patience.
